A workshop, a bakkie and one stubborn borehole.

Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions started when a farmer asked us to keep his pump running through a load shedding week. We have been building around that problem ever since.

Our story

The business began in 2019 in a rented workshop outside Thohoyandou, repairing diesel irrigation pumps for neighbouring farms. The same complaint came back every month: the pump was fine, but the fuel and the electricity account were eating the harvest.

We fitted our first solar array to a borehole pump in 2020 for a cooperative of eleven vegetable growers. It is still running. Since then we have moved from retrofits to complete systems, and from one district to installations across Limpopo and northern Gauteng.

We remain a small team by choice. Every system we sell is one we can service ourselves, and every farmer we install for can reach a technician by phone.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To empower small scale and emerging farmers with affordable, sustainable agricultural technology that improves crop yield and water security.

Our vision

To become a leading provider of accessible, renewable energy agricultural solutions across Southern Africa.

What we hold ourselves to

Quote the real price

Our quotes include delivery, installation, commissioning and the first service. If a site needs extra trenching we say so before you sign.

Leave skills behind

Every handover includes training for at least two people on the farm, so the system does not depend on one person being available.

Measure the water

We fit a flow meter on every installation. Water security is a claim we would rather prove than repeat.
