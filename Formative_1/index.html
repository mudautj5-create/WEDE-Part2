Welcome to Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions

We design and supply affordable, solar-powered irrigation and water management systems for small-scale and emerging farmers.

Our mission is to empower farmers with sustainable agricultural technology that improves crop yield and water security while reducing reliance on unpredictable rainfall and rising electricity costs.

Based in rural South Africa, we partner with agricultural cooperatives, government agencies, and NGOs to bring accessible renewable energy solutions to farmers across Southern Africa.
