Water management packages

Hardware is only half the saving. Our water management packages add the parts that decide how much you actually use.

  • Borehole yield testing and a written safe abstraction rate.
  • Tank sizing so you can irrigate through three overcast days.
  • Flow meters and a simple logbook for recording monthly use.
  • Irrigation schedules matched to your crop and planting dates.
  • Conversion of flood or hose irrigation to drip, bed by bed.

How an installation runs

  1. Site visit We measure your field, test the borehole and check where the sun falls across the day. No charge within 80 km of an office.
  2. System design and quote You receive a layout drawing, a daily water yield figure and one installed price. Valid for 30 days.
  3. Installation Most kits are installed in one to three days. We trench, mount, plumb and wire the full system.
  4. Commissioning and training We run the system with you, hand over the maintenance card and train at least two people on the farm.
  5. Service and support A free service at six months, then an optional annual plan at R950 a year that covers cleaning, inspection and priority callouts.
