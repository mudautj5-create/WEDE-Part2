Proposal 1: Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions

Organisation Overview Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions is a hypothetical agritech enterprise based in a rural farming region of South Africa. The organisation designs and supplies affordable, solar powered irrigation and water management systems for small scale and emerging farmers, helping them reduce reliance on unpredictable rainfall and rising electricity costs.
Mission and vision statements Mission is to empower small scale and emerging farmers with affordable, sustainable agricultural technology that improves crop yield and water security. Vision is to become a leading provider of accessible, renewable energy agricultural solutions across Southern Africa.

Target audience Small scale and emerging farmers, agricultural cooperatives and government/NGO partners such as the Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development (DALRRD) who fund or endorse sustainable farming initiatives.

Website Goals and Objectives Goals • Generate qualified enquiries for solar irrigation products and consultation services. • Educate visitors on sustainable, solar powered farming solutions through clear, accessible content. • Build credibility with government agencies and NGO partners considering the organisation for funded projects.
Key performance indicators (KPIs) • Number of product enquiries submitted per month. • Average monthly website traffic and page views. • Bounce rate on the homepage and services page target under 50%.

Current Website Analysis Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions does not currently have a website, its only online presence is a Facebook page used to share project updates. This is a strength in that it presents an opportunity to design a website from scratch using current best practice UX, but it is also a weaknes, as the organisation has no formal, search able web presence and cannot currently host detailed product information, an enquiry form or a downloadable brochure for funders.

Proposed Website Features and Functionality • Homepage: hero image of a solar irrigation installation, brief introduction and a clear call to action to enquire. • About Us: organisation history, vision and the team behind the technology. • Services/Products: detailed information on solar irrigation kits and water management packages. • Enquiry page: a form for product and service enquiries, including a request a quote option. • Contact page: contact details and a map showing more than one service location, plus a contact form.

Design and User Experience Colour scheme An earthy palette of deep green and warm brown, paired with a solar yellow accent colour, reflecting agriculture and renewable energy. The color palette combines deep green and warm brown for agriculture, paired with a bright solar yellow accent for renewable energy. This mix creates a natural, balanced and modern look.

Typography A clean, highly legible sans serif typeface for both headings and body text, to remain readable for visitors accessing the site on lower end mobile devices. Layout and design A simple, mobile first layout with generous spacing and large touch targets, since many target users will browse via smartphone in rural areas with limited connectivity.

User experience considerations Navigation is limited to five core pages with clear labels, the enquiry form is short and mobile friendly and images are optimised for fast loading on slower rural internet connections.

Technical Requirements • Domain: a .co.za domain to improve local search visibility and trust with South African users. • Hosting: South African based shared hosting for faster local load times and POPIA friendly local data storage. • Languages/frameworks: HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript for form validation and navigation interactivity.
